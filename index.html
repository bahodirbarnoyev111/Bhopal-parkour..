Bhopal Parkour

Run and leap from the Chowk rooftops, over the Taj-ul-Masajid minarets, across Upper Lake, through Van Vihar to the Boat Club at dusk. The course is one endless loop of 204 platforms. Every lap gets harder (stones start to crumble) and your laps go into the record table.

Move WASD or stick · Jump Space (press again to double jump) · Run Shift · Look mouse or drag · R last checkpoint · T restart from the beginning. Collect diyas on the way.
